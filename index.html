dingdongbeep!

dingdongbeep! is a mobile music room with toy instruments and artworks you can look at and play with. Adjust the twelve letter sliders to compose a tune. Select play to hear it, or generate for a new tune. Sound begins only after you interact. Navigation opens archived pages in a new tab.
